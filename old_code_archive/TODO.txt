1️⃣ Does combining DTW with monthly stats make sense?
Conceptually: yes. You are combining:
DTW distance → similarity of daily temperature-change patterns
Monthly mean → overall warmth
Monthly min/max → extremes
This gives you both:
Shape similarity (how the month evolves day by day)
Level & extremes (how hot/cold it actually was)
That’s often better than DTW alone for climate analysis.

Typical weights:
w_dtw = 0.5–0.7
w_mean = 0.1–0.2
w_min = 0.1
w_max = 0.1
Adjust based on what you care about.
